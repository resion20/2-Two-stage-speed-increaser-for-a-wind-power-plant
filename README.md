# 2-Two-stage-speed-increaser-for-a-wind-power-plant

A two-stage speed-increaser for a wind turbine: planetary first stage followed by a parallel wheel–pinion second stage. The work described the design and verified gears and bearings per ISO, and defined shafts, fits, and assembly details.

Main steps

Brief & ratios: established the duty and split the total ratio between the planetary and parallel stages.

Gears (ISO 6336): selected module/teeth and applied profile shift; checked bending and contact stresses; iterated to meet targets.

Shafts: derived load cases from gear forces; performed static and fatigue checks; controlled deflection and stresses at fillets/keyways.

Bearings (ISO 281): chose types/series; evaluated L10 life; specified fits, preload/clearance, and sealing.

Assembly & tolerances: defined gear/bearing fits, housing registers, alignment, and service access.

Validation: estimated efficiency and heat losses; conducted quick NVH/deflection sanity checks; outlined a BOM.

Results (at a glance)

Gearsets met ISO limits for bending and contact.

Shafts passed static and fatigue with allowable deflection.

Bearings achieved the target L10 life with defined fits/preload.

The architecture delivered the overall ratio with manufacturable modules and face widths.
